### what I do

- I build backend systems and enjoy the unglamorous parts — retries, queues, the stuff that decides whether things actually hold up
- I break my own projects on purpose to find out where they bend before real traffic does
- I solve algorithmic problems competitively, which is where the "make it fast and correct" instinct comes from
- Three directions, one goal: get better than yesterday
